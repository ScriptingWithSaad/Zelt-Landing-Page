# Original design and bundled libraries

This is an educational recreation of [Zelt](https://zelt.app/). The original story copy, 118-frame animation sequence, SVG illustrations and two product videos remain attributed to Zelt. This repository does not grant additional rights to those assets.

`assets/source-assets.json` records the original artwork URLs. All sequence frames remain at their original 1600×1000 resolution. The source videos remain in `assets/videos`; full-length, fast-start copies are served from `assets/optimized`.

- GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.12.5 are served locally. Copyright and license notices remain in the vendor files. See [GSAP licensing](https://gsap.com/standard-license/).
- Locomotive Scroll 5.0.1 is served locally and includes its Lenis 1.3.17 scrolling engine. MIT licenses are retained in `assets/vendor`.
- Montserrat is served locally as a variable font under the SIL Open Font License, included in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

[Locomotive documentation](https://scroll.locomotive.ca/docs) and [Lenis documentation](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) describe the scrolling engine and GSAP integration.
